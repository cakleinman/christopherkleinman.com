About

I started at the Motorcycle Mechanics Institute, served a two-year LDS mission, then headed to BYU and enlisted in the Army as a dental specialist. Six years and four schools later, I finished a philosophy degree at BYU — formal logic focus, military science minor, ROTC commission. Along the way I taught myself to build software and earned a FINRA Series 65. Now I'm a 1LT in the Utah National Guard, a business advisor at the Utah VBRC, and I ship products on the side because I can't stop building things.
