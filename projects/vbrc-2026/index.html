A QUICK CAVEAT

Some things still need
a real expert.

01
Regulated data

HIPAA, payment cards, payroll, anything the IRS wants — get those tools from someone whose name is on the line.

For example: we almost built a sign-on-screen tool for this talk — and didn't. Signatures and contracts belong with people whose name is on the liability insurance.

02
Customer-facing logins

A landing page is fine. A login form on the open internet is not. If strangers can poke at it, have it reviewed.

03
Expensive to be wrong

Tax math. Dosing. Anything where the wrong number costs real money. These need a licensed human in the loop.

These tools live inside your business. On your machine. For your eyes.

HOW TO ACTUALLY DO THIS

You're not learning to code.
You're learning to direct.

1
Pick a tool. Then try another. Claude Code · Cursor · Codex · Copilot · Gemini · ChatGPT — they're all great, and each one's a little different. Spend a month with one, then shop around.
2
Tell it what you want, in plain English. "Build me a single HTML file that takes a CSV and shows a dashboard."
3
Open the file. Try it. Tell it what to fix. It iterates with you. Like an assistant who never sleeps.
4
Your data stays on your machine. Nothing you saw today phoned home. The five tools sit in a folder on a laptop.
